# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: Players and families in Palma, Mallorca looking to join a handball club. They're browsing for a local sports community, evaluating whether this club fits their values and goals. Secondary: Local businesses exploring sponsorship opportunities.

## Product Purpose

The official website for Club Handbol Mata de Jonc, a handball club founded in 1994. The site exists to recruit new members and showcase the club's values, history, and community. Success looks like visitors filling out the contact form to inquire about joining.

## Positioning

A local handball club in Palma that prioritizes personal and sporting development through human values and a culture of effort—never giving up on a ball.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Contact form to inquire about joining the club
- Secondary CTA: Follow on Instagram for updates and community
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Tots Units Feim Força!" (Together We Make Strength!)
- Belief ladder: (1) This is a real, established club (founded 1994), (2) They care about values, not just wins, (3) I can see myself or my child fitting in here, (4) I should reach out and learn more
- Proof on hand: 30+ years of history, 100+ athletes, 50+ teams, sponsor logos (sponsor1-3.png, collaborator1-2.png)

## Brand Personality

Energetic, sporty, community-focused. The voice is enthusiastic but grounded—proud of tradition without being stuck in the past. Emotional goals: belonging, pride, determination.

## Anti-references

Generic sports templates with stock photos and corporate feel. Overly modern tech-startup aesthetics. Too formal or institutional presentations. The site should feel like a real local club, not a franchise.

## Design Principles

1. **Show the community, not just the sport** — Real people, real values, real history
2. **Pride without arrogance** — Celebrate achievements while staying approachable
3. **Local roots, modern presence** — Honor 30+ years of tradition while feeling current
4. **Action-oriented** — Every section should drive toward connection (contact, follow, join)
5. **Multilingual by default** — Catalan first, Spanish and English as equal alternatives

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Basic accessibility: semantic HTML, sufficient color contrast, keyboard navigation, responsive design. The site serves a broad community including families with children, so clarity and ease of use are prioritized.
